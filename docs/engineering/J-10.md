J-10 roadmap
We'll learn these concepts in order:
1. What exceptions are and why we need them.
2. try and catch.
3. Multiple catch blocks.
4. finally.
5. throw and throws.
6. Checked vs unchecked exceptions.
7. Exception propagation.
8. Custom exceptions.
9. Try-with-resources.
We'll use banking and payment examples throughout, so the concepts connect to the backend systems you eventually want to build.
